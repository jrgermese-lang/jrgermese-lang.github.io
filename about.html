WHO AM I?

My name is Jielian R. Germese. I've been passionate about art and design since I was a kid, experimenting with sculpture using modeling clay and digital 3D modeling. My journey into game design began in 2021 during the pandemic, and since then, I've been captivated in crafting immersive digital worlds. I aspire to one day create engaging video games and showcase my artworks in a gallery. My creative journey has been fueled by a love for crafting engaging game experiences and bringing imaginative concepts to life through sculpture. I believe that art has the power to inspire and connect people, and I'm excited to explore the limitless possibilities that both game design and sculpture offer.
